## Later: "Leicester"

Called several times, Nestor seemed asleep. The English recogniser wrote
"Nestor, Nestor, Nestor" as "Nestor Leicester Leicester", so it woke only
when one came through spelt right. `CalledName.soundsLike` now takes
"leicester", "lester" and "nesta" as the name ("nestle" stays out: a test
keeps "Nestle rang" from waking it). The cost: talk of Leicester wakes it.
The owner chose "Hey Nestor" as a second way to call it: the recogniser is
told to expect the phrase, and after "hey" a name two letters off counts
("hey Hastor"), while "hey Emma", "hey Peter" and "hey mister" do not.
Then "Ey Nestor" too, for a call without the h: "ey", "eh", "ay", "hay" and "hi" count like "hey"; "a" does not ("a pastor").

## Later: smoother conversation

The owner found it stopped suddenly, mostly when asking a question. Now: a
question left hanging on "and", "the", "um", "et", "euh" and the like waits
3 s instead of 1.6 s (`VoiceIO.pauseNeeded`); after an answer it waits 15 s
for the next question instead of 8 and ends with "I'm here when you need
me" rather than in silence; a slow answer is talked through
(`Progress`): "Let me think" after 3.5 s with nothing said, then a line for
each new kind of work as the agent starts a tool ("Searching for it",
"Reading it now", "Looking through your notes", mail, calendar, WhatsApp,
Slack; at least 6 s apart), then "Still on it", "It's coming", "Just a
little longer" after each 12 s with nothing new; talked over, the
voice fades in a quarter of a second instead of stopping dead. Not yet
tried by voice.
The timed "Let me think" at 3.5 s was dropped the same day: answers without
a lookup start 4 to 6 s after the question, so it spoke 0.6 to 2 s before
them (four turns from 13:04). Following the usual advice for voice agents
(speak only when the wait is real; let the model that picks the tool say the
line), the spoken prompt asks for one short sentence before any tool.
Checked headless: "two plus two" got only the answer; reading a paper got
"Let me look that up." first. The app's own tool lines stay as a fallback,
6 s after anything said, and "Still on it" after 12 s of quiet.
