# 2026-11-26b: quiet logs, "show your face", and a face

Continues [2026-11-26](2026-11-26.md).

## Quiet logs and window commands

- **Quiet logs** (`483c3a5`). The wake listener's routine lines are debug, so
  nothing is kept in the system log while it waits. It keeps no audio and
  writes no file. `cb9e81f` adds a debug line with the loudest microphone
  level every 5 seconds, to tell a silent microphone from a deaf recogniser.
- **"Concierge, show your face"** (`b4c0985`). `Conversation.windowCommand`
  handles short commands without asking the model: "show your face", "come
  up", "where are you" bring the window forward (`showWindow` reopens the
  app's own bundle, which also makes a window when there is none); "hide",
  "go away" hide the app. A sentence of more than six words is a question.

## The face

The owner asked for a face: "not too much a human face, humanoid or cartoon,
AI stuff, surprise me".

- **First try, rejected.** A dark robot head with glowing eyes and a mouth of
  seven dots. The owner saw it asleep, small and dim, and called it "a bit
  sinister. Bit cuter please." Changed to a pale shell, big round eyes with a
  sparkle, pink cheeks and a small curved mouth.
- **The owner then asked to choose**, for faces "from the most humanoid to
  more humans", for animals, and for places it could sit. Nine styles were
  drawn (`Face.Style`: halo, sprout, robot, kitty, android, bellhop, owl,
  penguin, pup) and five places were shown as small pictures of the window.
  A web search gave: a 1,064-person test where most people chose an abstract
  picture over a human-like one, human-like faces rated more attractive and
  more eerie, and placement advice not to cover content.
- **The owner chose "Owl, it moves"** (`492e1f4`, `6516da8`, `73abeaf`). The
  owl wears a concierge's small round hat. It rests in the top bar next to the
  date, where it replaces the microphone button and keeps Cmd-T. When a
  conversation starts it glides to the middle of the window and grows, with
  the heard words under it, and glides back at the end. When the window is
  out of sight the same face appears in a corner of the screen
  (`FacePanel.swift`, a panel that never takes the keyboard). All nine styles
  stay in Settings.
- **How it is built.** `Face.swift` is plain functions: expression from the
  conversation state, a pose per expression, blink and gaze from the clock,
  and how long until the next picture. `FaceView.swift` draws a pose in a
  style. The mouth follows the real loudness of the voice:
  `VoiceIO.loudness(of:)` measures the synthesised speech 30 times a second.
- **Cost.** Three ways of drawing the resting face were measured; see
  [tried.md](../tried.md). It ends at about 1% CPU at rest with the wake
  word on.

## A Talk bug found on the way

Clicking Talk ended the conversation after about a second whenever the wake
word was on. The wake listener lets go of the microphone as the conversation
starts, and the recogniser then ends the conversation's first turn with "No
speech detected". `VoiceIO.listen` now starts a fresh turn when a turn ends
early with nothing heard, until its own wait is over (`73abeaf`). The bug
dates from the wake word (`c4a4810`); the face made it visible because the
face invites a click.
