# 2026-11-28: the first job-runner page, and three picks

Started from a side session. The owner said to carry on with the picks and
to assume the Mac's lid is always shut, so the headset is the only
microphone.

## What was checked

- **The morning page from Nestor's own job runner worked.** The Mac slept
  through 07:20 and woke at 08:35. The runner built the page at 08:44 on
  Sonnet: 68 turns, 156 s, $1.39, seven sections, no section missing, every
  source read. ntfy holds "Morning page ready · nothing urgent", which
  matches the page.

## What was built, in order

1. **Nestor shows when it cannot hear** (`Hearing.swift`). After a minute of
   pure silence the face in the top bar holds a wing or paw to its ear, its
   tooltip says to switch the headset on, and one Mac notification goes
   out. The advice reads the lid (`AppleClamshellState`). Tried live with
   the headset off: deaf at 09:06:36, cleared at 09:07:37 when the owner
   switched it on. Not yet checked by the owner: the notification and the
   look of the face at its real size.
2. **A Cost History window** (`CostHistory.swift`), from the cost line's
   History link or Agent › Cost History. Seen live: five days, $17.33 in
   all, $3.47 a day.
3. **No more SDK pin.** Plain `swift build` works with Swift 6.4.2; tests
   and the release build pass without it, and the app runs.

## Later: the pages on the phone

The owner said yes, then chose the owner-only tailnet gate over an Authentik
login (nothing in the fleet puts Authentik before a plain page; ChemExtract,
Agent Watch and Spark jobs use the tailnet gate). The Mac's Docker was
stopped, so the Mac's backend Caddy was not used: `Scripts/pages.py` under
launchd listens on the Mac's tailnet address and checks the backend secret
itself. caddy-proxy `26c9b1b` adds `:39443`, validated in the running
container first, then a graceful reload on spark; the gate checker counts it
owner only, and the other apps answered as before. Checked: no secret or a
wrong one 403, spark calling the Mac directly 403, a POST 405, the run log
404, `/today` 200 with the no-fetch rule, and the page dark and readable at
phone width in Chrome. The owner's phone is a Pixel, so `/today` serves the
newest page in place and a manifest gives the home-screen shortcut its name
and icon. Not yet tried on the phone itself.
